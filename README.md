# EkAdhyapak Sahayak (एक अध्यापक सहायक)

**AI Teaching Assistant for Government Primary Schools with Only One Teacher**

Built for multi-grade classrooms (Classes 1–5) in resource-constrained government schools in India.

---

## Who is this for?

- Single-teacher primary schools (UDISE shows >1 lakh such schools)
- Teachers handling 3–5 grades together
- Schools with limited/no internet, few devices, Hindi + regional language medium
- Focus on Foundational Literacy & Numeracy (FLN) + NCERT curriculum

## How to use (3 steps)

1. Open `EkAdhyapak_Sahayak.html` in a browser (double-click or right-click → Open with).
2. Fill the simple form:
   - Topic / Chapter
   - Grades present today (e.g. 1,2,3,4)
   - Subject
   - Language preference (Hindi / English / Both)
3. Click any button → **Copy Prompt** → Paste into free AI (Grok.com, gemini.google.com, chatgpt.com) → Get ready materials in < 1 minute.

**No installation. No login. No paid subscription required.**

---

## Classroom Reality Tips

- Keep AI sessions short (5–10 min planning before school or during lunch).
- Always review AI output – especially for local culture, values, and accuracy.
- Prefer oral + blackboard activities over printed worksheets when paper is limited.
- Use the “Daily Timetable” feature every morning – it is the highest time-saver.
- For FLN: use the oral assessment prompts daily with 5–6 students.
