Daily Multi-Grade Checklist

  • 9:00 – Assembly + Attendance (all together)
  • 9:15 – Common introduction of today’s topic (5–7 min)
  • 9:25 – Grade 1–2: Simple activity / oral work
  • 9:25 – Grade 3–5: Written or higher activity (teacher monitors both)
  • 10:10 – Quick oral check with 4–5 students
  • 10:20 – Peer teaching (older help younger)
  • 10:40 – Mid-day meal / break
  • After meal – Story time or revision
  • Closing circle – What did we learn today?

Ready FLN Oral Questions

Reading (Class 1–2): 2–3 letter words, simple sentences from board.

Number Sense:

  • Count forward / backward from any number
  • Show me 7 fingers / 12 beads
  • Which is more: 15 or 19?
  • What comes after 29?

Class 3–4: 2-digit addition orally, simple word problems.

Group Work Ideas (Single Teacher)

  • Peer Teaching: Class 4–5 teach Class 1–2 a simple concept
  • Station Rotation: 3 corners – Reading, Maths, Drawing (10 min each)
  • Blackboard Leaders: One student from each grade writes answers
  • Story Chain: Teacher starts, each grade adds one sentence

Blackboard Tips

  • Divide board into 3 parts: Grade 1–2 | Grade 3 | Grade 4–5
  • Write the common topic big in the centre
  • Use simple drawings (sun, tree, house, stick figures)
  • Leave space for children to come and write
