Daily Multi-Grade Checklist

  • 9:00 – Assembly + Attendance
  • 9:15 – Common introduction of today’s topic
  • 9:25 – Grade 1–2 oral work / Grade 3–5 written work
  • 10:10 – Quick oral check
  • 10:20 – Peer teaching
  • Closing circle – What did we learn?

Ready FLN Oral Questions

Reading (Class 1–2): 2–3 letter words, simple sentences from board.

  • Count forward / backward from any number
  • Show me 7 fingers / 12 beads
  • Which is more: 15 or 19?
  • What comes after 29?

Group Work Ideas

  • Peer teaching: Class 4–5 help Class 1–2
  • Station rotation: Reading, Maths, Drawing
  • Blackboard leaders from each grade

Blackboard Tips

  • Divide board: Grade 1–2 | Grade 3 | Grade 4–5
  • Write the common topic in the centre
  • Simple drawings only (sun, tree, house)
